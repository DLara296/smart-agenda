# Domain Model

## Core Entities
### School
Represents a school with metadata such as name, location, timezone, and status.

### Grade
Organizes academic levels within a school and supports active/inactive configuration.

### Group
A grouping within a grade, such as reading circles or classroom cohorts.

### Teacher
An individual assigned to reading sessions by grade, language, and group responsibilities.

### Family
A household record that can include multiple adults and multiple children.

### Parent or Guardian
A family member with contact information, languages, and notification preferences.

### Child or Student
A child associated with a family, school, grade, and group.

### Reading Session
A scheduled reading event with date, time, group assignments, and communication metadata.

### Volunteer Assignment
A person-to-session assignment with a language, status, and history of updates or replacements.

### Notification
A communication event generated by the application, such as volunteer request, reminder, or teacher update.

## Relationships
- A school contains many grades.
- A grade contains many groups and students.
- A family contains many adults and children.
- A child belongs to one school/grade/group relationship within the relevant active period.
- A reading session belongs to a school and one or more groups.
- A volunteer assignment belongs to a session and a parent or guardian.
- A notification belongs to a session or assignment and can be retried or resent.

## State Model
### Session States
- Draft
- Scheduled
- Open for volunteer response
- Coverage confirmed
- Teacher notified
- Completed
- Cancelled

### Assignment States
- Requested
- Confirmed
- Cancelled
- Replaced
- Completed
- No-show

## Domain Rules
- A volunteer must be associated with a valid family record.
- Coverage validation must consider both group and language requirements.
- Cancellation must preserve history rather than deleting the original assignment.
- Rotation is configurable and must support overrides and skipped weeks.
